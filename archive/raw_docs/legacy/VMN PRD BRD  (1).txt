11. BROADCAST DOMAIN — MASTER BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS

BR-BCAST-001 — Channel Registry

Maintain canonical records for Nigezie Xtreme; Orisun — The Source; Isi Mbido — The Beginning.

BR-BCAST-002 — Channel Identity

Each channel must support a Channel ID; brand name; approved editorial proposition; target audience; languages; genres; channel owner; carriage endpoints; scheduling timezone; verified technical parameters; and active/inactive status. Unverified details remain marked as such.

BR-BCAST-003 — Programme Catalogue

Maintain, or integrate the authoritative existing record for, every relevant Broadcast programme and version.

BR-BCAST-004 — Schedule Management

Every scheduled programme must occupy a controlled schedule slot. Approved schedules and subsequent changes have revision identities.

BR-BCAST-005 — EPG

Generate or manage Electronic Programme Guide metadata from approved scheduling and governed editorial metadata, preserving their source references.

BR-BCAST-006 — Commercial Traffic

Link advertising and sponsorship inventory to schedules, campaigns and delivery obligations.

BR-BCAST-007 — Playlist / Playout

Approved schedules must produce playout instructions or playlists compatible with the actual Broadcast workflow. This does not require replacement of functioning automation.

BR-BCAST-008 — As-Run Evidence

Retain source evidence of what actually aired, including deviations, missing items and reconciliation decisions.

BR-BCAST-009 — Transmission Monitoring

Transmission failures and material deviations must create owned operational incidents.

BR-BCAST-010 — Carriage Management

Maintain verified StarTimes and future carriage relationships, agreements, endpoints and contacts.

BR-BCAST-011 — Rights and Exploitation Gate

A programme, asset or version may be proposed for planning while rights are under review, but must remain a visibly BLOCKED CANDIDATE outside the approved, exportable schedule.

No schedule approval, executable playlist export, transmission, Azeebo+ publication, social exploitation or TEND delivery may proceed unless rights and applicable compliance requirements for the specific intended use are verified.

The gate evaluates:

The underlying instrument and evidence.

Property, asset and technical/editorial version.

Channel, destination, platform and medium.

Territory.

The entire intended time and exploitation window.

Repeat limits.

Exclusivity.

Promotional use and other applicable conditions.

Current revision and withdrawal status.


Record decision source, owner, revision and time. A later rights change, withdrawal or relevant schedule edit invalidates the affected decision and triggers reevaluation at the next controlled gate.

No scheduler, operator, Channel Manager or system administrator can create a right through an override. Legal/IP may correct an erroneous rights record, accept newly verified permission or resolve an evidence hold within delegated authority. It must retain the instrument, evidence, old and new state, actor, reason and time in the authoritative rights source before reevaluation. Unknown, disputed, revoked, expired or prohibited use remains blocked. Emergency replacement content must be independently pre-cleared.

BR-BCAST-012 — Technical Master Gate

No file-based asset enters transmission without the required technical approval.

BR-BCAST-013 — Compliance Gate

Applicable Broadcast, content and advertising compliance status must be visible and enforced at the relevant gate.

BR-BCAST-014 — Sponsorship

Support programme, segment, daypart and channel sponsorship.

BR-BCAST-015 — Advertising Inventory

Expose available and booked spot inventory from its authoritative source.

BR-BCAST-016 — Cross-Platform Campaigns

Support governed campaigns spanning Broadcast, Azeebo+, creators, events and social.

BR-BCAST-017 — Broadcast-to-Azeebo Acquisition

Approved scheduled programmes may carry verified deep links, QR codes or calls to action into Azeebo+.

BR-BCAST-018 — Content Reuse

Broadcast content may route to Azeebo+, social or TEND only where the rights for that separate use permit it.

BR-BCAST-019 — Broadcast Revenue

Advertising, sponsorship, carriage-related economics and other channel commercial activity must reconcile through Finance. This BRD/PRD does not set accounting treatment.

BR-BCAST-020 — Proof of Delivery

Commercial airtime and sponsorship commitments require appropriately classified and verifiable proof of delivery, including owned exception handling for missed, partial or disputed obligations.

15. PROGRAMMING & SCHEDULING

Scheduling must support:

A 24-hour grid.

Dayparts.

Premieres.

Repeats.

Strip scheduling.

Weekly scheduling.

Block programming.

Special events.

Live programmes.

Filler.

Promos.

Station idents.

Commercial breaks.

Emergency replacement content.


Normal schedule states are:

DRAFT → PROGRAMMING REVIEW → RIGHTS CHECK → TRAFFIC READY → APPROVED → PLAYLIST GENERATED → ON AIR → AS-RUN RECONCILED → ARCHIVED

A DRAFT may contain a blocked candidate for planning. That candidate cannot pass RIGHTS CHECK or reach an approved/exportable schedule, executable playlist or transmission. Rights, technical master, commercial and compliance controls apply at their relevant stages.

An approved schedule has a revision identity. Every exportable playlist and EPG export identifies the source schedule revision and applicable programme/asset versions. A later approved change creates a new revision; it does not overwrite earlier approval or what actually aired. Changed rights, commercials, promos and timing are revalidated at the affected gate.

Emergency continuity records the authorised substitution and preserves the planned-versus-actual difference. “ON AIR” and “AS-RUN RECONCILED” depend on actual operational evidence rather than a manually advanced planning flag.

62. USER STORY — BROADCAST SCHEDULING

US-BCAST-001 — Rights-safe scheduling

As a Programme Scheduler, I want the schedule system to show whether a programme’s rights cover its intended channel, medium, territory and full planned window so that I can plan without exporting or transmitting unauthorised content.

Acceptance criteria

1. The scheduled candidate links to a controlled programme asset/version and authoritative RightsRecord, or clearly records unresolved rights.


2. The system evaluates channel, medium, territory, complete window and relevant conditions against the current instrument and revision.


3. Expired, missing, unknown, disputed, revoked or inapplicable rights produce a reasoned BLOCKED CANDIDATE state.


4. A blocked candidate cannot enter an approved/exportable schedule or executable playlist.


5. The Scheduler cannot clear the hold by UI status change, direct API call, another role or a post-approval schedule edit.


6. Legal/IP may correct an evidence or record error or enter newly verified permission within delegated authority. The instrument and old/new decision, actor, time and reason remain recorded before reevaluation.


7. Rights changes and relevant schedule revisions trigger reevaluation at the applicable approval/export gate.


8. Emergency replacement uses separately pre-cleared content and records the operational decision and as-run result.


9. Regression tests cover expired rights, mid-run expiry, blocked draft, direct API attempt, verified record correction and emergency replacement.



The original R1.2 acceptance phrase “authorised conditional override requires named approver” is replaced. No approval in a user interface creates absent exploitation rights. If a proposed exception’s legal effect is unclear, the item remains blocked pending a Legal/IP decision.

Release	Applicable interpretation

Public channel information	BG0, approved editorial material and verification of facts actually published; no unsupported claim of live schedules or Azeebo+ integration

A limited rollout cannot waive a rights, payment, restricted-data, compliance or transmission control affecting its active scope. NOT TESTED is never represented as PASS. The authorised human release owner records GO, GO WITH CONDITIONS, HOLD or NO-GO against a fixed version, environment and evidence set.

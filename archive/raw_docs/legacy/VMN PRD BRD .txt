0. REBASE STATEMENT

R1.1 established the correct Creative Economy and Azeebo+ architecture but materially underrepresented VMNL’s existing broadcast business.

R1.2 corrects that omission.

The canonical VMNL enterprise is not:

> Creative Economy + Azeebo+.

> BROADCAST + CREATIVE ECONOMY + PRODUCTION + EVENTS + DISTRIBUTION + DIRECT-TO-CONSUMER + TECHNOLOGY + TALENT + PROPRIETARY IP.

BR-BCAST-001 — Channel Registry

The platform shall maintain canonical records for:

Orisun — The Source;

Isi Mbido — The Beginning.


BR-BCAST-002 — Channel Identity

Each channel must have:

brand name;

editorial proposition;

target audience;

languages;

genres;

channel owner;

carriage endpoints;

scheduling timezone;

technical parameters;

active/inactive status.


BR-BCAST-003 — Programme Catalogue

The system shall maintain every broadcast programme and version.

BR-BCAST-004 — Schedule Management

Every scheduled programme must occupy a controlled schedule slot.

BR-BCAST-005 — EPG

The platform shall generate/manage Electronic Programme Guide metadata.

BR-BCAST-006 — Commercial Traffic

Advertising and sponsorship inventory shall be linked to schedules and campaigns.

BR-BCAST-007 — Playlist / Playout

Approved schedules must produce playout instructions/playlists compatible with the live broadcast workflow.

BR-BCAST-008 — As-Run Evidence

The system must retain evidence of what actually aired.

BR-BCAST-009 — Transmission Monitoring

Transmission failures must create operational incidents.

BR-BCAST-010 — Carriage Management

The system shall maintain StarTimes and future carriage relationships.

BR-BCAST-011 — Rights Gate

No programme enters broadcast schedule outside its permitted rights.

BR-BCAST-012 — Technical Master Gate

No file-based asset enters transmission without technical approval.

BR-BCAST-013 — Compliance Gate

Applicable broadcast/content/advertising compliance status must be visible.

BR-BCAST-014 — Sponsorship

Programme, segment, daypart and channel sponsorship must be supported.

BR-BCAST-015 — Advertising Inventory

The platform shall expose available and booked spot inventory.

BR-BCAST-016 — Cross-Platform Campaigns

Campaigns may span broadcast + Azeebo+ + creator + event + social.

BR-BCAST-017 — Broadcast-to-Azeebo Acquisition

Scheduled programmes may carry approved deep links, QR codes or calls to action into Azeebo+.

BR-BCAST-018 — Content Reuse

Broadcast content may route to Azeebo+, social or TEND only where rights permit.

BR-BCAST-019 — Broadcast Revenue

Advertising, sponsorship, carriage-related economics and other channel revenues shall reconcile through Finance.

BR-BCAST-020 — Proof of Delivery

Commercial airtime and sponsorship commitments require verifiable proof-of-air/delivery.

22. BROADCAST COMPLIANCE

The Broadcast domain must maintain an activity-specific compliance register including NBC broadcasting obligations applicable to VMNL’s services. NBC continues to publish the Nigeria Broadcasting Code and licensing/service-level materials for broadcasting services. 

Where films/video works fall within NFVCB’s classification mandate, their classification/approval status should be attached to the asset before applicable exhibition/distribution. NFVCB states that films and video works intended for distribution/exhibition in Nigeria are subject to its classification framework. 

The operating compliance register should also map applicable advertising, copyright, consumer, privacy, employment, tax and contractual requirements.
